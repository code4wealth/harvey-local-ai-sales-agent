# SAMPLE — FieldNest fictional company information

This document describes a fictional product for offline development and evaluation. It contains no customer claims, performance statistics, or real company information.

## Product
FieldNest is a fictional maintenance-planning board for small fabrication and food-processing workshops. It helps a local team record routine equipment checks, maintenance tasks, and shift handoffs.

## Intended value
A maintenance coordinator can keep a visible list of upcoming checks and the notes left by the previous shift. The product is intended for teams that currently coordinate these tasks with paper lists or separate spreadsheets.

## Features
- Equipment list with locally entered identifiers.
- Recurring maintenance checklist entries.
- Task owner and due-date fields.
- Shift notes attached to a maintenance task.

## Boundaries
FieldNest does not predict failures, monitor equipment sensors, order parts, or send email. It has no verified customers or measured outcomes. Any unknown fact should be stated as not provided.
