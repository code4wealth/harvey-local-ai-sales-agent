# SAMPLE — RelayCue fictional company information

This document describes a fictional product for offline development and evaluation. It contains no customer claims, performance statistics, or real company information.

## Product
RelayCue is a fictional browser-based workspace for B2B software support teams. It organizes case handoffs, internal notes, and links to approved support procedures in one queue view.

## Intended value
A support lead can see the next owner and the current internal context when a case moves between teammates. RelayCue is meant for teams that coordinate support work across shifts or specialist groups.

## Features
- Case handoff queue with owner and status fields.
- Internal notes attached to a case.
- Links to a team's own support procedures.
- A simple history of ownership changes.

## Boundaries
RelayCue does not send email, make decisions for agents, guarantee response-time improvements, or include customer references. Any unknown fact should be stated as not provided.
